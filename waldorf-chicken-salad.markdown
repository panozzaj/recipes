# Waldorf Chicken Salad

Based on [AllRecipes Chicken Salad with Apples, Grapes, and Walnuts](https://allrecipes.com/recipe/222912/chicken-salad-with-apples-grapes-and-walnuts/)

## Ingredients

- 4 cooked chicken breasts, shredded
- 2 medium Granny Smith apples, cut into small chunks
- 2 cups chopped walnuts
- 1/2 red onion, chopped
- 3 stalks celery, chopped
- 3 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1/2 cup vanilla yogurt
- 5 tablespoons creamy salad dressing (such as Miracle Whip)
- 5 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 25 seedless red grapes, halved

## Directions

1. Toss together chicken, apples, walnuts, red onion, celery, and lemon juice in a large bowl.
2. Whisk together yogurt, salad dressing, and mayonnaise in a small bowl. Pour over chicken mixture and stir to coat.
3. Gently fold in grapes.

## Notes

- Great way to use leftover BBQ or rotisserie chicken.
- Serves 12. Prep time about 25 minutes.
