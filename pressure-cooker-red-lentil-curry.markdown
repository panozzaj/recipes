# Pressure Cooker Red Lentil Curry

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp coconut oil (or butter/oil)
- 1 onion, diced
- 2-3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch ginger, minced (or ginger paste)
- 1+ cup red lentils
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 1 can fire-roasted crushed tomatoes (or diced)
- 1/2 to 1 can coconut milk
- ground cumin
- ground coriander
- turmeric or garam masala
- salt, black pepper

## Directions

1. In Instant Pot, sauté onion in coconut oil with cumin, coriander, and turmeric/garam masala until softened.
2. Add garlic and ginger, cook 1 minute.
3. Add lentils, chickpeas, tomatoes, and coconut milk. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Pressure cook 10 minutes on high, natural release.
