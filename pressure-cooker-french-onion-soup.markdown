# Pressure Cooker French Onion Soup

Based on [Serious Eats Pressure Cooker French Onion Soup](https://www.seriouseats.com/pressure-cooker-french-onion-soup-recipe)

By J. Kenji López-Alt

## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, plus more for toasts
- 3 lbs yellow or mixed onions, sliced 1/8 inch thick (4-5 large)
- 2 teaspoons Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup dry sherry (such as Amontillado)
- 2 quarts low-sodium chicken stock
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cider vinegar
- 16 bowl-size slices rustic bread, toasted
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 lb Gruyère cheese, grated
- Fresh chives, for garnish

## Directions

1. Melt butter in pressure cooker over medium heat. Add onions without stirring. Season with salt and pepper. Seal and cook at high pressure for 20 minutes. Natural release (about 30 minutes).
2. With lid off, cook stirring constantly until liquid has reduced and onions are golden brown, about 5 minutes. Add sherry, simmer until alcohol smell is mostly gone, about 3 minutes.
3. Add stock, thyme, and bay leaf. Bring to simmer, cook 10 minutes. Add vinegar, season to taste. Discard thyme and bay leaf.
4. To serve: Preheat broiler. Butter toasts and rub with garlic. Spoon broth into ovenproof bowls, add toasts, top with Gruyère. Broil until cheese is melted and browned, about 5 minutes. Garnish with chives.

## Notes

- Serves 8. Total time about 90 minutes.
- Mixed onions (yellow, sweet, red, shallots) produce more complex flavor.
- Recipe used to call for baking soda to help caramelize onions faster, similar to the cauliflower soup trick.
