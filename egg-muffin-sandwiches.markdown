# Egg muffin sandwiches

This makes 2 sausage egg sandwiches, like your favorite breakfast place. But better.

Partly inspired by [Meal in a Mug](https://www.amazon.com/Meal-Mug-Recipes-People_All-Microwave/dp/1476798141)

### Ingredients

 - oil or butter
 - 2 eggs
 - 4 breakfast sausage links or 2 breakfast sausage patties
 - 2 English muffins
 - cheese (sharp white cheddar preferred)

### Instructions

1. Lightly coat a microwave-safe mug with some oil (avocado preferred) at the bottom and the bottom sides. Crack 2 eggs into it. Pierce egg yolks with fork to prevent explosion (important!). Cover with folded paper towel
2. Wrap 4 frozen pork sausages in 2 sheets of paper towels
3. Microwave covered mug and sausages for 222s @ 40% power (50% has the risk of blowing up, in my experience)
4. While microwaving, slice 2 English muffins cross-wise. Put one of them in the toaster and put the other on top of it, inside side down. When it pops, reverse them and toast again.
5. Jiggle eggs in microwave mug and sausages again at 222s @ 40% power
6. Get out sharp white cheddar cheese and slice off 6 slices
7. Carefully remove mug from microwave and check that the eggs are done.

Now everything is cooked.

Assemble sandwiches. The order is helpful to keep the sandwich from sliding around and to melt the cheese well.

1. Put muffin bottom down
2. Optional: add roasted red pepper hummus or avocado / guacamole to bottom
3. Pat sausages dry and slice lengthwise, putting flat side down on muffin
4. Place layer of cheese (3 slices)
5. Remove eggs from mug. May have to slide butter knife around edge. If difficult, use more oil the next time.
6. Slice egg in half, cross wise. It will be hot. It will slide less if you place on the paper towel.
7. Place middle portion of egg (steamiest) on top of cheese
8. Place muffin top
9. Press gently on both sandwiches to meld the ingredients, melt cheese, steam muffin, and make it easier to eat

Enjoy! Once you've practiced, takes about eight minutes.
