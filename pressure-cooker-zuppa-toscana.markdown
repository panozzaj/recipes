# Pressure Cooker Zuppa Toscana

Based on [Delish Copycat Olive Garden Zuppa Toscana](https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/recipes/a45873/copycat-olive-garden-zuppa-toscana-recipe/)

Olive Garden-style Italian sausage and potato soup.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb hot Italian sausage, casings removed
- 4 slices bacon, chopped
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4-6 cups chicken broth
- 4 large russet potatoes, diced
- 1 bunch curly kale, stems removed, chopped
- ¾ cup heavy cream
- Salt and pepper
- Parmesan for serving

## Directions

1. Set Instant Pot to sauté. Brown sausage and bacon, breaking up sausage. Remove and set aside.
2. Sauté onions in the rendered fat until soft. Add garlic, cook 1 minute.
3. Add sausage, bacon, potatoes, broth, salt and pepper.
4. Pressure cook on high for 5 minutes. Natural release 10 minutes, then release remaining steam.
5. Add kale, stir well, cover and let heat 5 minutes until wilted.
6. Stir in heavy cream. Season to taste, garnish with Parmesan.

## Notes

- 6 cups broth may be too much - start with 4 cups and adjust.
- Stovetop: simmer potatoes in broth 20-25 min until tender instead of pressure cooking.

## My Notes
