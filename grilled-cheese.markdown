# Grilled Cheese

Tips and variants for grilled cheese sandwiches.

## General Tips

- Use a lighter bread
- Toast the inside in toaster on bagel mode, or in toaster oven/air fryer stacked
- Serve with tomato soup or red pepper and tomato soup

## Basic Grilled Cheese

Butter, bread, cheese. You know the drill.

## Jalapeño Popper Grilled Cheese

- Cream cheese on base (warm it up or microwave 5-10 seconds to spread easier)
- Jalapeño peppers, sliced small
- Cheddar
- Optional: bacon or other meat
- Optional: egg added to melted butter for top

## Reuben Style

- Sauerkraut
- Corned beef
- Swiss cheese

## Tomato and Ham

- Tomato slices
- Horseradish
- Ham

## Fried Cheese Outside

Inspired by Punch Burger - put shredded cheese on the outside of the bread and fry until crispy.

## Kenji Weighted Grilled Cheese

Press with a weight (cast iron pan) while cooking for extra crispy/flat result.
