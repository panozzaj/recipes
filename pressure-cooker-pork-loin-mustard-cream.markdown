# Pressure Cooker Pork Loin with Mustard and Cream

From "The Great Big Pressure Cooker Book" by Bruce Weinstein & Mark Scarbrough

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 (2½ lb) boneless pork loin
- 4 large shallots, peeled and halved
- ¼ cup dry, light white wine (such as Sauvignon Blanc)
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- 1 teaspoon dried dill
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- ⅓ cup heavy cream

## Directions

1. Melt butter with oil in pressure cooker on sauté/browning. Brown pork loin on all sides, turning occasionally, about 6 minutes. Transfer to plate.
2. Add shallots to pot, cook stirring occasionally until lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
3. Pour in wine and scrape up browned bits. Add broth, stir in mustard, dill, and pepper.
4. Return pork loin to pot with any juices from plate.
5. Lock lid. Cook at high pressure 18 minutes (stovetop) or 27 minutes (electric).
6. Quick release. Transfer pork to cutting board, cool 5 minutes. Keep lid loosely on cooker to keep sauce warm.
7. Slice pork into ¼-inch rounds.
8. Gently warm cream in small saucepan over low heat. Whisk into sauce in cooker.
9. Serve pork rounds in bowls with shallots and plenty of sauce.

## Notes

- Nothing like cream to set off braised pork - but add at end since cream breaks under pressure.
- Can use light cream or half-and-half instead.
- Serves 4-6.

## My Notes

- Can use regular onions if no shallots, but shallots are better.
