# Roasted Carrot and Cauliflower with Turmeric

A colorful, flavorful side dish. Also good on the grill.

## Ingredients

- 1 head cauliflower, cut into chunks and then sliced lengthwise
- 1 lb carrots, cut into thirds then halved or quartered depending on thickness
- 1 onion, cut into wedges
- 4-5 cloves garlic, smashed
- olive oil
- salt, pepper
- turmeric
- cumin
- paprika
- Victoria Taylor's Toasted Sesame Ginger (or similar blend with sesame, ginger, garlic, red pepper)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Put cauliflower in a large bowl, then layer carrots on top. Add olive oil and spices, then toss. This ensures the carrots get more spice - otherwise the cauliflower absorbs it all.
3. Spread on sheet pan and roast for 30 minutes.
4. Add garlic and onion wedges, toss, and roast another 20-30 minutes until everything is tender and edges are browned.

## Notes

Can also be done on the grill using a grill pan with holes.
