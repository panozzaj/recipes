# Pressure Cooker Chicken and Rice Casserole

From "The Great Big Pressure Cooker Book" by Bruce Weinstein & Mark Scarbrough

With artichokes and tomatoes.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- ½ lb mild or hot Italian sausage, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 6 boneless skinless chicken thighs (about 1½ lbs), trimmed
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 2 small green bell peppers, stemmed, cored, chopped
- 2 teaspoons minced garlic
- ⅓ cup rosé wine, preferably Spanish
- 1 (28 oz) can whole tomatoes, drained and roughly chopped (about 3½ cups)
- 1½ cups chicken broth
- 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons long-grain white rice (such as white basmati or Texmati)
- 1 (9 oz) box frozen artichoke heart quarters, thawed (about 2 cups)
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper

## Directions

1. Heat oil in pressure cooker on sauté/browning. Add sausage and cook, turning occasionally, until browned, about 4 minutes. Transfer to bowl.
2. Add chicken and brown well, turning once, about 6 minutes. Transfer to bowl.
3. Add onion and bell peppers to pot, cook stirring often until onion is translucent, about 4 minutes. Add garlic and cook until aromatic, 1 minute.
4. Pour in wine and bring to simmer, scraping up browned bits. Continue simmering until reduced to thick glaze.
5. Stir in tomatoes, broth, rice, artichoke hearts, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and pepper. Return sausage to pot, nestling chicken pieces into liquid, making sure rice is submerged.
6. Lock lid. Cook at high pressure 10 minutes (stovetop) or 15 minutes (electric).
7. Quick release. Set cooker aside for 10 minutes to steam the rice.
8. Stir gently before serving.

## Notes

- Rice should absorb almost all liquid - if some remains when you open pot, set to sauté and cook off liquid.
- Good enough for a dinner party.
- Serves 4-6.
