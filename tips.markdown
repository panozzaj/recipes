# Cooking Tips

## General

- Sear, then cook, for flavor
- If cooking in oil and want browning, reduce water content by drying out first
- Use salt. Salt early, especially meat. Taste. Kosher salt is nice - have a few boxes on hand since it's cheap and never goes bad
- Use acid to brighten up food, including heavy meals
- Cook aromatics first. Add onion first (high water content, takes longer). Garlic burns easily - add last, just a minute or two before liquid, or with heat off
