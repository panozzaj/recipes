# Sheet Pan Shrimp Boil

A low country boil-style meal. Great for groups since everyone can season their own portion.

## Ingredients

- Shrimp, peeled and deveined
- Small potatoes, quartered
- Corn on the cob, halved or cut into rounds (fresh or defrosted from frozen)
- Smoked sausage (such as andouille or kielbasa), sliced
- Garlic, minced
- Butter, melted
- Lemon juice and wedges
- Old Bay seasoning
- Salt and pepper
- Dill
- Thyme
- Paprika

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Divide potatoes, corn, sausage, and garlic among individual oven-safe containers.
3. Season each container to taste with butter, lemon juice, Old Bay, salt, pepper, dill, thyme, and paprika.
4. Roast for about 20 minutes.
5. Add shrimp to each container, drizzle with more butter. Roast another 8-10 minutes until shrimp are pink and cooked through.
6. Serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes

- Individual oven-safe containers let everyone customize their own seasonings.
- Can also put everything in at the same time, but adding shrimp second prevents overcooking.
- Works well as a camping recipe using a cast iron skillet over the fire or camp stove.
- The butter and lemon are key - don't skip them.
