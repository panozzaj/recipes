# Pressure Cooker Cauliflower Soup

From "The Great Big Pressure Cooker Book" by Bruce Weinstein & Mark Scarbrough

Buttery, ridiculously creamy cauliflower soup - uses baking soda trick.

## Ingredients

- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter, cut into bits
- 1 medium cauliflower head (about 1½ lbs), cored, trimmed, broken into 2-inch pieces
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon dried dill
- ⅛ teaspoon grated nutmeg
- ¼ teaspoon baking soda
- 2½ cups vegetable or chicken broth

## Directions

1. Melt butter in pressure cooker on sauté/browning. Stir in cauliflower, salt, caraway, and baking soda until combined. Pour ¼ cup water over everything.
2. Lock lid. Cook at high pressure 10 minutes (stovetop) or 15 minutes (electric).
3. Quick release.
4. Use immersion blender to puree soup in pot, or transfer in batches to blender.

## Notes

- The baking soda helps break down the cauliflower for an ultra-creamy result.
- Can substitute ½ tsp ground caraway for the dill/nutmeg.
- Serves 6.
