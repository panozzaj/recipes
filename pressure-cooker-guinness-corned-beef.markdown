# Pressure Cooker Guinness Corned Beef

Based on [The Kitchen Whisperer](https://www.thekitchenwhisperer.net/2017/03/15/pressure-cooker-guinness-corned-beef-with-cabbage-potatoes-carrots/)

## Ingredients

- 3 lb flat cut corned beef, patted dry
- Pickling packet (from corned beef)
- 3 Tbsp yellow mustard
- 1 Tbsp whole grain mustard
- 1 (14-16 oz) bottle Guinness draught (about 11 oz)
- 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1 head cabbage, cut in 1" wedges, core removed
- 4-8 carrots, cut into 2-3" pieces
- 6-8 red potatoes, skin on, cut in half

## Directions

1. Mix together the mustards and pickling packet.
2. Place corned beef fat side up in pressure cooker with onions along sides.
3. Spread mustard mixture over top of fat on corned beef. Pour beer and Worcestershire sauce along sides.
4. Sprinkle salt and pepper over top.
5. Pressure cook on high for 90 minutes. Natural release.
6. Remove corned beef, place on plate and cover loosely with foil.
7. Add cabbage, carrots, and potatoes to pot. Pressure cook on high for 3-4 minutes. Quick release.
8. Remove vegetables with slotted spoon.
9. Slice corned beef against the grain.

## Guinness Reduction Gravy (optional)

- Reserved cooking liquid, strained
- 1 Tbsp - 1/4 cup brown sugar (to taste)
- 2 Tbsp grainy mustard
- 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 tsp horseradish
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1/2 cup honey

Bring all ingredients to boil in pot using sauté function. Cook until reduced by half.
