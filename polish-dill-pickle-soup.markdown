# Polish Dill Pickle Soup

Based on [Salt & Lavender Polish Dill Pickle Soup with Smoked Ham](https://www.saltandlavender.com/polish-dill-pickle-soup-smoked-ham/)

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 cup finely chopped dill pickles
- 3 medium carrots, grated and chopped
- 6 large red potatoes (skins on), diced
- 2 (10 oz) cans chicken broth
- 1.5 cups pickle juice
- 6 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- Handful fresh dill, chopped
- 3/4 lb smoked ham, diced
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1 tablespoon flour
- Bay leaf
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Directions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add onion and carrot, sauté 5 minutes.
2. Add remaining ingredients except sour cream and flour. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to medium-low, simmer 15-20 minutes until potatoes are tender.
3. Mix flour into sour cream until smooth. Stir into soup and cook another 5 minutes.
4. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with extra fresh dill.

## Notes

- Recipe above is doubled from original.
- Serves 6.
