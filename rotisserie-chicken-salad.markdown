# Rotisserie Chicken Salad

## Ingredients

- chicken
- mayonnaise
- spicy mustard
- celery, diced
- celery salt
- salt
- pepper
- paprika
- cajun seasoning
- lemon juice
- dill (fresh or dried)

## Directions

1. Shred or dice cooked chicken.
2. Mix with mayonnaise, mustard, and lemon juice.
3. Add celery and seasonings.
4. Adjust to taste.
