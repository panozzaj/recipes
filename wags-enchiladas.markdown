# Wags Enchiladas

Family recipe from my friend Adam Wagner.

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 3 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp Mexican-style chili powder
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 5 tbsp flour
- ~3 cups water
- pinch of salt

### Filling (choose one)

- cooked, shredded chicken
- ground beef
- spinach and black beans

### Assembly

- 5 inch corn tortillas
- cheese

## Directions

### Sauce

1. In a large skillet, melt butter.
2. Add chili powder, garlic, and flour. Mix until no clumps.
3. Add water and salt, simmer until sauce thickens.
4. Consider making a double batch - the sauce is that good.

### Assembly

1. Layer tortillas, filling, and sauce in a baking dish (or roll individually).
2. Bake covered at 325°F for about 20 minutes, until heated through.
3. Uncover, top with cheese, and return to oven until melted. Can use broiler, but watch carefully to avoid burning.
