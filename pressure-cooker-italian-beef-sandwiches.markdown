# Pressure Cooker Italian Beef Sandwiches

## Ingredients

- 5 Italian sausages
- 1 green pepper, sliced
- 1 onion, sliced
- ~3 lb chuck beef, cut into chunks
- garlic
- 1 package Italian seasoning mix
- 1 cup beef broth
- pepperoncinis
- 1 cup pepperoncini juice

## Directions

1. In Instant Pot on sauté, fry sausages for a few minutes.
2. Add green pepper and onion, fry briefly.
3. Remove sausages and veggies, set aside.
4. Sizzle beef chunks in the pot.
5. Add garlic, Italian seasoning, beef broth, pepperoncinis, and pepperoncini juice.
6. Return sausages and veggies to pot.
7. Pressure cook 60 minutes on high, natural release.

## Notes

Serve on crusty rolls with provolone or other cheese, with the cooking liquid for dipping.
