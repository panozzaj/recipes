# Pressure Cooker Rice Custard

From "The Great Big Pressure Cooker Book" by Bruce Weinstein & Mark Scarbrough

## Ingredients

- 2⅔ cups whole or 2% milk
- 2 large eggs plus 2 large egg yolks, at room temperature
- 2 cups cooked long-grain white rice
- ⅓ cup sugar
- ½ cup golden raisins
- 1½ tablespoons vanilla extract
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Zest of 1 orange or lemon

## Directions

1. Butter the inside of a 2-quart round, high-sided soufflé or baking dish. Set a rack inside a 6-quart pressure cooker; pour in 2 cups water.
2. Whisk the milk, eggs, and egg yolks in a large bowl until smooth and creamy. Whisk in the rice, sugar, raisins, vanilla, salt, cinnamon, and zest; pour into the prepared baking dish. Cover tightly with aluminum foil.
3. Make an aluminum foil sling and set the baking dish on top. Use the sling to lower the dish onto the rack in the cooker.
4. Lock lid. Cook at low pressure 38 minutes (stovetop) or high pressure 30 minutes (electric).
5. Natural release 8-12 minutes.
6. Use the foil sling to transfer the baking dish to a wire rack. The custard should be loosely set, thickened but still jiggly. Cool for 1 hour before serving.

## Notes

- Serves 6.
- Raisins will swell under pressure - chop them first if that bothers you.

## My Notes

- Added cinnamon and citrus zest to the original recipe.
- Other dried fruit or nuts (almond slivers, etc) are good additions.
- Could also use [pumpkin spice](./pumpkin-spice-usage.markdown) instead of cinnamon.
