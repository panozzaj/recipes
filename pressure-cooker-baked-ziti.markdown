# Pressure Cooker Baked Ziti

Based on [Together as Family Instant Pot Baked Ziti](https://togetherasfamily.com/perfect-instant-pot-baked-ziti)

## Ingredients

- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 cups heavy whipping cream
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 box (16 oz) ziti pasta
- 1 can (15 oz) crushed or diced tomatoes
- 2 tsp dried basil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 cup shredded parmesan cheese

## Directions

1. Spray Instant Pot insert with cooking spray.
2. Pour in chicken broth first, then heavy cream. (Important: broth must go first.)
3. Add salt, pepper, and garlic.
4. Add pasta - DO NOT STIR. Press down gently if needed but don't stir.
5. Pour crushed tomatoes evenly over pasta. Sprinkle basil and oregano on top. DO NOT STIR.
6. Pressure cook on high for 5 minutes. Natural release 10 minutes, then quick release.
7. Add parmesan cheese and stir everything together until combined.
8. Serve with additional parmesan and fresh basil if desired.

## Notes

- Diced tomatoes work fine instead of crushed. Prefer fire roasted.
- Serves 8. Total time about 17 minutes.
- Made this dozens of times - very forgiving recipe, basically always turns out well.
