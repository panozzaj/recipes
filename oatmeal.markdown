# Oatmeal

## Method

1. Boil water in a kettle.
2. While waiting, toast oats in a pot.
3. Add boiling water to cover oats.
4. Cover and cook on medium-low heat until done.
5. Add cream or milk toward end.

## Tips

- Use regular oats (not steel cut or quick cooking)
- Half oats, half granola is a good ratio
- Cook fruit in a separate pan
- Can add granola for easy add-ins (raisins, nuts, etc.) - adds sugar though

## Fruit Ideas

- Apples with pumpkin spice or cinnamon
- Berries simmered in orange juice

## Variations

Sweet potato version: cooked sweet potato, warm spices, sliced almonds, coconut milk, and maple syrup.
