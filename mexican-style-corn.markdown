# Mexican-Style Corn

## Grilled Corn (Elote-Style)

Based on [NYT Grilled Corn Mexican Style](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1013216-grilled-corn-mexican-style)

### Ingredients

- corn on the cob
- mayonnaise
- queso fresco
- lime juice
- chili powder (Lime Tajín or cayenne work well)

### Directions

1. Grill corn until charred in spots.
2. Spread with mayonnaise.
3. Sprinkle with queso fresco, lime juice, and chili powder.

### Variations

- Add fresh peaches when in season - wonderful summer treat.

## Charred and Raw Corn Salad

Based on [Bon Appétit Charred and Raw Corn with Chile and Cheese](http://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/charred-and-raw-corn-with-chile-and-cheese)

### Ingredients

- corn (some charred, some raw)
- red bell peppers
- jalapeño (optional, for extra kick)
- cotija or similar cheese
- lime juice
- cilantro

### Directions

1. Char some corn kernels in a hot pan or on grill.
2. Cut remaining corn raw off the cob.
3. Dice peppers.
4. Combine all ingredients with cheese, lime juice, and cilantro.

### Notes

Made with red peppers - quite good. Could add an additional jalapeño for more heat.
