# Pressure Cooker White Chicken Chili

Based on [Natasha's Kitchen Instant Pot White Chicken Chili](https://natashaskitchen.com/instant-pot-white-chicken-chili-recipe/)

## Ingredients

- 2 large chicken breasts (frozen is fine)
- 15 oz can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 15 oz can white beans, drained
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 10 oz can Rotel diced tomatoes with green chilis (with juice)
- Chicken broth (enough to just cover)
- 1/2-1 tsp chili powder (or omit for kid-friendly)
- 1 tsp cumin
- 0.4 oz packet ranch dip or dressing mix (about 2 tsp)
- 8 oz cream cheese, cut into pieces

## Directions

1. Add chicken, beans, onion, tomatoes with juice, broth, cumin, chili powder, and ranch packet to Instant Pot.
2. Pressure cook on high for 25 minutes. Quick release.
3. Remove chicken to plate and shred with two forks.
4. Add cream cheese to pot, stir to melt.
5. Return shredded chicken and stir to combine.

## Toppings

- Cilantro
- Diced avocado
- Tortilla chips

## Notes

- Corn is optional - can omit.
- Can use frozen chicken.

## My Notes

- Use chicken thighs instead of breasts.
- Use 1 can each: black beans, white beans, chickpeas.
- Use Mexican oregano and cumin instead of ranch packet and chili powder.
