# Roasted Broccoli Soup

## Ingredients

- broccoli
- garlic
- chicken stock
- 2-3 tbsp pesto

## Directions

1. Roast broccoli and garlic at 400°F until slightly browned.
2. Bring chicken stock to a simmer.
3. Add roasted broccoli and garlic to stock.
4. Blend with immersion blender.
5. Stir in pesto.
