# Pressure Cooker Cheeseburger Soup

From "The Great Big Pressure Cooker Book" by Bruce Weinstein & Mark Scarbrough

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 large yellow onion, chopped
- 1 medium bell pepper (prefer orange), stemmed, cored, chopped
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 3 cups chicken broth
- ¾ cup dry white wine (such as Chardonnay)
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- ½ teaspoon dried marjoram
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 pound lean ground beef (at least 93% lean)
- ½ cup heavy cream, light cream, or half-and-half
- 2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese, preferably sharp (about 8 oz)

## Directions

1. Melt butter in pressure cooker on sauté/browning. Add onion and bell pepper, cook stirring often until softened, about 4 minutes.
2. Whisk in flour and cook 1 minute, whisking constantly until vegetables are coated but flour doesn't brown. Pour in broth in slow stream, whisking to dissolve flour. Add wine and whisk until bubbling and thickened, about 2 minutes.
3. Stir in thyme, marjoram, salt, and pepper. Crumble in ground beef in little chunks.
4. Lock lid. Cook at high pressure 5 minutes (stovetop) or 8 minutes (electric).
5. Quick release. Set pot to sauté/browning. Stir in cream, simmer 1 minute stirring constantly. Add cheese and stir until melted. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Serves 4-6.
- Important to whisk in step 2 to keep flour from browning.

## My Notes

- Marjoram is not a common spice but worth picking up for this.
- Have only made this a few times.
