## Overnight Crockpot Oatmeal

- 8 C water
- 2 C steel cut oats
- pinch of salt
- cook on low overnight (8-10 hours)
- might add a bit of butter to help it not stick, although this hasn't made a significant difference

### Add-ins

I like adding some tbsps of peanut butter, apple sauce, cinnamon or other warm spices (allspice, clove, etc. - I usually use pumpkin spice since I get it on sale from Kroger after fall), and raisins or other dried fruit.

### Toppings

Can top with nuts, cut up fruit, chia seeds, etc. If you add milk or cream, add it right at the end or before serving since otherwise it cooks too long.
