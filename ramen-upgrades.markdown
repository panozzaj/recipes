# Ramen Upgrades

Ways to improve packaged ramen.

## Base

Get better base ramen - Nongshim Shin Ramyun is good.

## Method

Boil water in kettle, put seasoning packet(s) in microwave-safe bowl (usually not all the seasoning), add the ramen puck, pour boiling water over. Microwave about 3 minutes.

## Add-ins

- egg (crack in as last add-on, or add soft-boiled after cooking)
- vegetables: green onions, mushrooms, spinach, kimchi, carrots, corn
- cooked protein (chicken, pork, beef, fish) - add at end to heat through
- gochujang, sriracha, garlic, ginger
- sesame seeds, sesame oil (balances flavor)
- cheese, seaweed
