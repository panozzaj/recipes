# Remoulade Sauce

[Serious Eats Louisiana Remoulade](https://www.seriouseats.com/sauced-louisiana-remoulade-recipe) is a good base.

## Ingredients

- mayonnaise
- sour cream (less than half as much as mayo)
- spicy or whole grain mustard
- lemon juice
- hot sauce (Frank's or Louisiana-style like Crystal)
- pickle or caper brine
- couple of capers, minced
- salt, black pepper

## Directions

Mix all ingredients to taste.

## Serving

Great on [salmon cakes](./salmon.markdown#leftovers).
