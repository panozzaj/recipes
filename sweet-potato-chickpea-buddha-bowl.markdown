# Sweet Potato Chickpea Buddha Bowl

Based on [Minimalist Baker Sweet Potato Chickpea Buddha Bowl](https://minimalistbaker.com/sweet-potato-chickpea-buddha-bowl/)

## Ingredients

### Vegetables

- 2 Tbsp olive, melted coconut, or avocado oil
- 1/2 medium red onion, sliced in wedges
- 2 small sweet potatoes, halved
- 1 bundle broccolini, large stems removed, chopped
- 2 big handfuls kale, larger stems removed
- 1/4 tsp each salt and pepper

### Chickpeas

- 1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and patted dry
- 1 tsp cumin
- 3/4 tsp chili powder
- 3/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp each salt and pepper
- 1/2 tsp oregano (optional)
- 1/4 tsp turmeric (optional)

### Tahini Sauce (optional)

- 1/4 cup tahini
- 1 Tbsp maple syrup
- 1/2 medium lemon, juiced
- 2-4 Tbsp hot water, to thin

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Arrange sweet potatoes and onions on a bare baking sheet. Drizzle both with oil, making sure the flesh of the sweet potatoes is well coated. Place skin side down.
2. Bake for 10 minutes, then flip sweet potatoes and add broccolini. Drizzle broccolini with oil and season with salt and pepper.
3. Bake for another 8-10 minutes, then add kale. Drizzle kale with a touch more oil and season with salt and pepper. Bake for another 4-5 minutes, then set aside.
4. While vegetables are roasting, heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add chickpeas to a mixing bowl and toss with seasonings.
5. Add 1 Tbsp oil to the hot skillet with chickpeas and sauté, stirring frequently. If browning too quickly, turn down heat. If not much browning, increase heat. About 10 minutes at slightly over medium heat.
6. Once chickpeas are browned and fragrant, remove from heat and set aside.
7. Prepare sauce by whisking tahini, maple syrup, and lemon juice in a bowl. Add hot water until a pourable sauce forms.
8. To serve: slice sweet potatoes into bite-size pieces. Divide vegetables between serving bowls and top with chickpeas and tahini sauce.

## Notes

- Used asparagus instead of broccolini - worked well.
- Used hummus instead of tahini sauce.
- Could be more filling - consider adding more chickpeas.
- Liked the chickpeas especially. Recipe is easy since it's just throwing a bunch of stuff in the oven at the right times.
- Serves 3. Vegan and gluten-free. Best fresh, but keeps 3-4 days in the fridge.
