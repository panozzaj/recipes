# Slow Cooker Cheater BBQ Pork

Two "cheater" pulled pork recipes that use a slow cooker and liquid smoke instead of a smoker.

## Cheater Basic Dry Rub

Makes about 2/3 cup. Used in both recipes below.

- 1/4 cup paprika
- 2 tablespoons kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons coarsely ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon dry mustard

---

## Molasses Vinegar Pork Butt

Based on [Food.com Molasses Vinegar Pork Butt](https://www.food.com/recipe/molasses-vinegar-pork-butt-373565)

### Ingredients

- 3/4 cup molasses
- 1 cup cider vinegar
- 1/2 cup liquid smoke
- 5-6 lb boneless Boston butt (pork shoulder)
- 1/4 cup Cheater Basic Dry Rub (see above)

### Directions

1. Pour molasses into bottom of large slow cooker (at least 5 quarts). Add vinegar and liquid smoke.
2. Cut pork butt into 2-3 inch chunks and add to slow cooker.
3. Sprinkle meat with dry rub, turning pieces to coat evenly.
4. Cover and cook on HIGH for 5-6 hours or LOW for 10-12 hours until meat is pull-apart tender and reaches 190°F internally.
5. Transfer meat to rimmed platter using tongs and slotted spoon. Let cool until handleable.
6. Pull meat into strands and serve with warm cooking juices.

### Notes

- Yields 12-14 servings.
- Refrigerate cooled meat and cooking juices separately.
- Skim congealed fat layer before reheating.

## Ultimate Cheater Pulled Pork

Based on [The Splendid Table Ultimate Cheater Pulled Pork](https://www.splendidtable.org/story/2013/12/12/ultimate-cheater-pulled-pork) from "Cheater BBQ" by Mindy Merrell and R.B. Quinn

### Ingredients

- 5-6 lb boneless Boston butt or boneless country-style pork ribs
- 1/4 cup Cheater Basic Dry Rub (see above)
- 1/2 cup liquid smoke
- Barbecue sauce of your choice

### Directions

1. Cut pork into 2-3 inch chunks (ribs don't need cutting).
2. Place meat in large slow cooker (5+ quarts), sprinkle with dry rub while turning to coat evenly, add liquid smoke.
3. Cook covered on HIGH for 5-6 hours or LOW for 10-12 hours until meat reaches 190°F internally and shreds easily.
4. Transfer meat to platter using tongs and slotted spoon. Let cool, then pull into strands.
5. Serve on buns with barbecue sauce.

### Oven Alternative

1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Place whole pork butt in large roasting pan and coat generously with dry rub.
3. Pour liquid smoke into pan.
4. Cover pan tightly with heavy-duty foil.
5. Roast 5-8 hours until pull-apart tender and 190°F internally.

### Notes

- Yields 12-14 servings.
- True "dump and cook" recipe with minimal prep.
- Reheat gently on stovetop with reserved juice, or in covered dish at 350°F for 20-30 minutes.
